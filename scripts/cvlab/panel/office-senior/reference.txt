TO WHOM IT MAY CONCERN

Julie Tanner – Office Manager

Julie Tanner worked for this practice as Office Manager from June 2010 until February 2017, when she left us to take up a post nearer her home in Bristol.

Julie ran our office of 22 people single-handedly and, I think it is fair to say, better than it had ever been run. She looked after our invoicing and purchase ledger, prepared the payroll information for our accountants each month and managed all of our suppliers. When she joined, our clients were taking around two months to pay us; by the time she left it was closer to six weeks, which made a real difference to a practice of our size. She also organised our move to new offices in 2014, which we completed over a weekend without losing a working day.

She is discreet, unflappable and extremely well organised, and she was trusted by everyone here, from the partners to the newest Part I student. She trained and supervised our office junior with patience and good humour.

We were very sorry to lose her. I recommend her without reservation and would be happy to speak to any prospective employer.

Yours faithfully,
