## 1. What cookies and browser storage are

A cookie is a small piece of text a website asks your browser to keep and send back on your next visit. Browser storage is the same idea, kept in your browser for the website itself to read. A service worker is a small script your browser keeps, so a website can show you notifications. The law treats them all the same way. In this policy, "cookies" means all three.

## 3. Why we don't ask for your consent

The EU ePrivacy Directive (Article 5(3)) requires a website to ask your consent before it stores or reads anything on your device, except when that is strictly necessary to provide a service you asked for. In Hungary the same rule is in the Electronic Communications Act (Act C of 2003, section 155(4)), and Hungary's data protection authority (NAIH) applies the same exception.

Everything in the table above is in that exception. Each one either keeps you signed in, remembers a choice you made, makes a feature you turned on work, or protects you and us from fraud and attacks. None of them measures you, follows you to other websites or is used for advertising. So the law doesn't require a consent banner, and we don't show one.

## 4. What we don't use

- No analytics or statistics tools, such as Google Analytics.
- No advertising, retargeting or "pixel" trackers.
- No social media buttons or embedded videos that track you.
- No fonts, scripts or images loaded from other companies to show our pages. Our fonts are on our own server.
- No recording of what you do on the page.

## 5. Cookies set by other companies

**Stripe** sets its cookies only through its payment form, which Mahoraga loads on the page where you pick a plan and pay. They help Stripe stop payment fraud. Stripe's own [cookie policy](https://stripe.com/cookies-policy/legal) explains them.

**Cloudflare**, which carries all of Mahoraga's traffic, may set a cookie to tell people from bots and protect the site from attacks. Cloudflare's own [cookie policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/cookie-policy/) explains it.

## 6. How to control them

You can see and delete cookies and site data in your browser's settings. Deleting Mahoraga's cookies signs you out. Blocking them stops sign-in from working, because they are what keeps you signed in. You can turn notifications off in Mahoraga, on the Alerts page, or in your browser's settings for the site.
