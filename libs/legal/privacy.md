## 3. What we collect, why, and on what legal basis

**Your account.** Your email address; your password, stored only as a one-way hash (argon2id) that can't be turned back into your password; your sign-in sessions; your time zone; when you last used Mahoraga; and which version of our terms you agreed to, and when. *Why:* to run your account and keep it secure. *Legal basis:* our contract with you (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).

**What you're looking for.** The words, places, languages and ways of working you choose; your alerts; your Jobs page filters; the jobs you skip, bookmark, add by their link or mark as applied; and the progress of your applications, if you record it (interview, offer, hired, rejected). *Why:* to show you the jobs that match and send your alerts. *Legal basis:* contract.

**Your documents.** The CV and cover letter files you upload and their text; your name and the links you add (About you); and the CVs and cover letters we make for you. *Why:* to write your documents, work out match scores and suggest words for your search. *Legal basis:* contract.

**Your match scores and suggestions.** Worked out by fixed rules from your documents and your search, not by AI. They only sort and filter your own list. No decision about you is ever made by them, and no one else sees them. *Legal basis:* contract.

**Your linked accounts.** The sign-in or API key of the AI account you link, and the token of your own Telegram bot, each encrypted with a key that belongs to your account alone. They are never shown back, not even in part. *Legal basis:* contract.

**Notifications.** The address your browser gives us when you turn notifications on, your Telegram chat, and the notices in Mahoraga's bell. *Legal basis:* contract. Your browser asks for your permission before it shows any notification, and you can turn notifications off at any time.

**Payments.** For a paid plan: the plan, the state of your subscription, its trial and renewal dates, and the date and amount of your last payment, as Stripe reports them. Your card details go to Stripe and never reach us. *Legal basis:* contract, and our legal obligations under tax and accounting law (Article 6(1)(c)).

**Messages to us.** What you write through a contact form or by email, your email address, and our replies. *Why:* to answer you, and to keep a record of complaints, as Hungarian consumer law requires. *Legal basis:* our legitimate interest in answering you (Article 6(1)(f)), and that legal obligation.

**Technical information.** Your IP address is used, only in our server's memory, to limit how often something can be done (such as signing up), and is not stored by us. Our server logs record what the system did, with the internal number of the account involved, and occasionally an email address when an email to it fails. *Why:* to keep Mahoraga secure and working, and to find faults. *Legal basis:* our legitimate interest in a secure, working service.

**Cookies and browser storage.** See the [Cookie Policy](#/cookies).

**Improving Mahoraga.** We count, across everyone, how far applications got for documents made in each layout and with each version of our instructions to the AI, to learn which work better. We look at the totals, not at any one person. *Legal basis:* our legitimate interest in making documents that work, which doesn't override your interests because only totals are used. You can object (section 10).

**Sensitive information.** We don't ask for information about your health, religion, ethnic origin, political opinions, trade union membership or sexual orientation, and a CV doesn't need it. If you choose to put it in your documents, we use it only to write your own documents, on the basis of your explicit consent, given by including it (Article 9(2)(a)). You can withdraw that consent by removing it from your documents at any time.

We collect ads from public job boards and companies' own career pages, and show them as published, so that people can find and apply for jobs. *Legal basis:* our legitimate interest, and the interest of job seekers, in finding published jobs (Article 6(1)(f)). ## 10. Your rights

You have the right to:

- **access** your data, and get a copy of it (Article 15);
- have it **corrected** if it's wrong (Article 16);
- have it **erased** (Article 17): delete your account in Settings, or ask us;
- **restrict** how we use it while a question about it is settled (Article 18);
- **take it with you** in a machine-readable format (Article 20): see the [Data Use Policy](#/data);
- **object** to our use of it based on legitimate interests (Article 21), and we'll stop unless we have compelling reasons that override yours;
- **withdraw consent** at any time, where we rely on it, without affecting what was done before (Article 7(3));
- not be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing that significantly affects you (Article 22). Mahoraga makes no such decision.

Most of these you can do yourself in Mahoraga. For anything else, write to [{email}](mailto:{email}) from the email address on your account. We'll answer within one month. If a request is complex, we may extend that by two more months, and we'll tell you why within the first month. It's free, unless a request is clearly unfounded or excessive.

## 12. Keeping your data safe

All traffic to Mahoraga is encrypted (HTTPS), and our server can only be reached through Cloudflare. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes. Linked accounts and bot tokens are encrypted with a separate key for each account, which is itself encrypted with a key kept outside the database. Only the people who run Mahoraga can reach its data. The [Data Use Policy](#/data) explains more, including what we do if something goes wrong.

## 13. Children

Mahoraga is for people aged 16 and over. If you believe a child under 16 has given us their data, tell us and we'll delete it.
