# Data Use Policy

> **The short version**
>
> - Jobs come from public job boards and companies' own career pages. We show where each one came from.
> - Your jobs are picked and ranked by fixed rules, not by AI, and nobody but you sees how.

## 1. What this page is for

The Privacy Policy sets out your rights over your personal data and the legal basis for each use. This page explains, in practical terms, how Mahoraga uses data: the jobs it collects, the rules that match them to you, the AI that writes your documents, how you can take your data elsewhere under the EU Data Act and the GDPR, and how we keep it safe.

## 2. Where the jobs come from

2.1 Mahoraga reads job ads that are published openly, on pages anyone can open without signing in: job boards, companies' own career pages and job feeds. It does this automatically, many times a day, the way a search engine does. It never signs in to other websites.

2.2 Each job shows where it came from, and its "Apply on" button takes you to that website. The ad belongs to whoever published it. We don't write or change job ads, apart from putting them in a consistent layout.

2.3 We can't check every ad. An ad may be out of date, wrong, or, rarely, not genuine. See section 5.3 of the Terms of Service.

2.4 Jobs stay on Mahoraga for {shelf} after we find them. After that, we keep them in an archive, so that the jobs you applied for stay in your list, and so we can see how the job market changes. If an ad names you, the Privacy Policy explains your rights (section 4 there).

## 3. How your jobs are picked and ranked

3.1 A job joins your list when one of your words is in it, none of your excluded words is in its title or company, and it is in a place, language and way of working you chose. These are fixed rules, the same for everyone, that you can see and change in My search.

3.2 The match score, from 1 to 10, compares the job ad with your CV using fixed rules: the role, your experience and skills, and what the job requires. It is not AI and doesn't learn from you. It only sorts and filters your own list, and only you see it.

3.3 No employer sees your match scores, your search or your list, and nothing in Mahoraga decides anything about you. You decide what to apply for.

## 4. How AI is used

4.1 **For one thing only:** writing a CV or cover letter for a job, when you press the button to make one.

4.2 **With your own AI account.** It is written by the provider of the AI account you link in Settings: Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (ChatGPT) or Google (Gemini). We don't run AI on our own account for anything you can use, and we don't run our own AI models.

4.3 **What is sent, and when.** When you press the button, and only then, we send your provider the text of the documents you uploaded and switched on in My documents (such as your CV, cover letters and what you wrote about yourself), your name, the languages of the jobs you're looking for, and the job ad, with our instructions for writing the document. Whatever your documents say goes with them, including any contact details in your CV. Nothing else is sent: not your account's email address, your password, your other search settings or your list of jobs.

4.4 **What your provider does with it.** It writes the document and sends it back. What it keeps, and whether it may use what it received to improve its AI, depends on your agreement with it and your settings there. See section 7 of the Privacy Policy.

4.5 **What happens next.** We turn the document into a PDF on our own server, and check by code, not by AI, that its text can be read back whole and in order, as the software employers use to sort applications reads it. The document is yours to check and change before you send it: AI can make mistakes (section 6 of the Terms).

4.6 **No AI learns from your data.** We don't train, fine-tune or improve any AI model with your data, and we don't let anyone else do so through us.

## 5. What we never do

- Sell, rent or trade your data.
- Share your data with employers, recruiters or data brokers.
- Show you adverts, or build a profile of you for advertising.
- Use your CV or documents for anything other than your own documents, match scores and word suggestions.
- Train AI with your data.

One thing we do to improve Mahoraga: we count, across everyone, how far applications got for documents made in each layout and with each version of our instructions. We only look at the totals (Privacy Policy, section 3).

## 6. Your data is yours: taking it with you

6.1 **What you can take.** Everything you gave us, and everything we made for you: your account details, search, alerts, uploaded files and their text, the documents we wrote, your job lists and the progress of your applications. It doesn't include what is ours (Mahoraga's software, its matching rules and its instructions to the AI) or anything about other people.

6.2 **How.** You can download every document we've made for you in My documents, at any time. For a complete copy of everything else, write to [{email}](mailto:{email}) from your account's email address. We'll send it in a common machine-readable format (JSON) within 30 days, usually sooner, free of charge. You can give it to any other service, or ask us to send it to one directly where that is technically possible.

6.3 **Switching to another service.** The EU Data Act gives customers of data processing services the right to switch provider easily. Wherever those rules apply to Mahoraga, this is how we meet them, and we offer the same to everyone anyway:

- You can leave at any time. A paid plan ends at the end of the month you've paid for, and you can delete your account straight away. There is no notice period.
- There is no charge for leaving, switching or taking your data, now or later.
- Within 30 days of your request, we send you your data as described in section 6.2, and help with any reasonable question about it.
- Cancelling a paid plan doesn't delete anything: your data stays in your free account for as long as you want it there. When you delete your account, everything is erased at once, apart from backups, which are replaced on the schedule in the Privacy Policy.

6.4 Mahoraga is not a connected product, such as a smart device, so the Data Act's rules on data from connected products don't apply to it.

## 7. How we keep your data safe

- **In transit:** every connection to Mahoraga is encrypted (HTTPS). Our server has no open door to the internet: it can only be reached through Cloudflare.
- **Passwords:** stored only as one-way hashes (argon2id), which can't be turned back into your password.
- **Linked accounts and bot tokens:** encrypted (AES-256-GCM) with a key belonging to your account alone. That key is itself encrypted with a key kept outside the database, so a copy of the database alone can't unlock them. They are never shown back to anyone.
- **Sign-in:** sessions run out, and changing your password signs you out everywhere. Sign-up and sign-in are limited, to slow down anyone guessing.
- **Payments:** card details go straight to Stripe and never reach us.
- **Access:** only the people who run Mahoraga can reach its data, and only to run and support it.
- **Backups:** made every night and checked after each one, so a failure doesn't lose your data. Copies kept away from our server are encrypted before they leave it.

## 8. If something goes wrong

If a security incident puts personal data at risk, we report it to Hungary's data protection authority (NAIH) within 72 hours of finding out, unless it is unlikely to harm anyone. If it is likely to put you at high risk, we tell you directly and without delay: what happened, what it means for you, and what we're doing about it.

## 9. Requests from authorities

We hand over data to an authority only when the law requires us to, and only what it requires. We check that each request is lawful, and we tell you about it unless the law forbids us to.
